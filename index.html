who we are

We are a small farm. We grow vegetables, share the harvest and keep the gate open.

The land is for people.

We look after it together, and we try to leave it better than we found it.

Nobody goes hungry here.

For food shares you give what you can. If you have nothing to give, you still eat.

Knowledge travels.

Seeds, recipes and know-how come from many places. We share them freely.

Food is not a commodity.
